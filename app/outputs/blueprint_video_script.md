# Blueprint — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover. Timestamps assume a steady, confident pace — practice once before recording so the cuts land close to these marks.

---

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

**[SCREEN: Blueprint landing page, particle background]**

> "Every team project starts the same way: a group chat to find people, a spreadsheet for tasks, a Drive folder nobody can find, and a GitHub repo half the team isn't even in. Five tools before you've written a line of code.
>
> This is Blueprint — one app that finds your team, then runs the whole project."

---

## 0:20–0:45 — Sign in & matching

**[SCREEN: Sign in with Google → land in a project's Matches tab]**

> "Sign in once with Google or GitHub, and Blueprint already knows who you are. Post the roles your project needs, and it ranks real candidates by fit — skills, headline, match score — not just whoever saw the group chat first."

**[ACTION: scroll through ranked candidate cards, point out match % and skill tags]**

> "See someone promising? Send a message request right from their card — subject, message, done. They get it in their mailbox, bottom right, the second they're online."

**[ACTION: click Message on a candidate, show the compose modal, send it, cut to the mailbox badge]**

---

## 0:45–1:15 — The project workspace

**[SCREEN: Switch into a project — Overview tab]**

> "Once your team's set, Blueprint doesn't hand you an empty room. Every new project auto-provisions a shared Google Drive folder and a GitHub repo — everyone's in, nobody has to be manually invited."

**[ACTION: show Drive/GitHub links in the sidebar]**

> "Tasks live on a Kanban board you can drag between columns."

**[ACTION: quick drag of a task card from To Do to In Progress]**

---

## 1:15–2:10 — Chat, the real center of gravity

**[SCREEN: Team chat tab]**

> "But the real hub is chat. Full team roster with live online/offline status on the right. Every message can be replied to, reacted to, or translated on the spot if a teammate's working in a different language."

**[ACTION: hover a message, show the Reply/Translate/React popup; click one]**

> "Search finds anything that's ever been said."

**[ACTION: type into the chat search bar, show filtered results]**

> "Record a voice note and watch a live spectrogram while you talk — no layout jump, no guessing if it's picking up your mic."

**[ACTION: hold the mic button, show the waveform bars moving, release to send]**

> "Drop in a file — even a 3D model — and it renders right in the browser. STL, OBJ, STEP, FBX, whatever your CAD tool exports."

**[ACTION: attach an STL, click it, show the orbiting 3D viewer]**

---

## 2:10–2:35 — Everything else, fast

**[SCREEN: Quick cuts — Calendar, Whiteboard, Video call]**

> "Calendar syncs with Google Calendar in both directions. A shared whiteboard for when a chat message isn't enough. Video meetings without leaving the tab."

**[ACTION: 2-second cut each: calendar month view → whiteboard with a quick sketch → video call tile grid]**

---

## 2:35–2:50 — Polish that makes it feel real

**[SCREEN: Account preferences — theme + color picker]**

> "Light or dark mode, a fully custom accent color picker, a guided tour for anyone's first time in — every detail built from scratch, not a component library skin."

**[ACTION: drag the saturation/hue picker, watch the accent color change live across the UI]**

---

## 2:50–3:00 — Close

**[SCREEN: Back to the landing page / logo]**

> "Find your team. Run your project. All in one tab. This is Blueprint."

**[END CARD: Blueprint wordmark + your GitHub/demo link]**

---

## Notes for recording

- Keep the cursor moving deliberately — pause half a second after every click so the action reads clearly at normal playback speed.
- If you're short on time when editing, the safest cuts to shorten are the 2:10–2:35 "everything else" block (already fast) and trimming pauses, not the chat section — that's the part with the most built functionality to show off.
- Mute notification sounds/other tabs before recording so nothing unrelated pops up on screen.
